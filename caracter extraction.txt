Veo 3.1 Prompt Guide
The Formula
Shot  →  Subject  →  Action & Dialogue  →  Environment   →  Mood & Audio
Components
Shot
• Camera style — handheld, steady cam, drone
• Shot type — close-up, medium, wide
• Movement — tracking, static, panning
Subject
• Age, build, distinctive features
• Clothing — specific items, colors, fit
• Posture, movement, expression
Action & Dialogue
• Specific movements and interactions
• Dialogue in direct quotes — She says: "Just keep walking."
• Secondary characters and their reactions
Environment
• Location — street, alley, plaza, metro
• Background — crowd, traffic, ambient activity
• Props, signage, weather, time of day
Mood & Audio
• Overall tone — tense, melancholic, electric, gritty
• Implied sounds — traffic, voices, rain, music
Quick Tips
• Be specific. "Worn leather jacket" beats "casual clothes".
• Use active verbs. Describe what you see happening, not what it feels like.
• Layer foreground to background. Main subject first, then environment.
• Add contradiction. Unexpected details create visual tension and interest.

Common Elements to Include
Character ages and physical descriptions
Specific clothing items and colors
Facial expressions and body language
Camera movement and positioning
Lighting conditions and quality
Background activity and crowd reactions
Props and environmental details
Emotional undertones and energy levels
